What we do

Five programme divisions, three launch programmes

Each division has a distinct purpose and audience but a shared commitment to public-interest digital trust. At launch, the Foundation’s work is delivered through three connected programmes designed to protect the citizen, validate the infrastructure and strengthen the ecosystem.

Programme divisions

The Foundation’s standing capability

Phase 1 establishes the DPI Trust Lab, the Open Digital Commons and Software Trust Division and the Policy and Evidence Centre. Phase 2 adds the Skills and Implementation Academy and the Public-Interest Cyber and Digital Safety Centre.

01 · Phase 1

DPI Security, Safety and Trust Lab

Safeguards research and assurance for the systems that hold identity, money and entitlement.

Division detail 02 · Phase 1

Open Digital Commons and Software Trust Division

A neutral home, a security baseline and a sustainability plan for digital public goods.

Division detail 03 · Phase 1

Digital Trust Policy and Evidence Centre

Translating technical risk into evidence that policy, regulation and investment can act on.

Division detail 04 · Phase 2

Digital Trust Skills and Implementation Academy

Capability that stays in the country after the engagement ends.

Division detail 05 · Phase 2

Public-Interest Cyber and Digital Safety Centre

Cyber support for the organisations that protect people but cannot afford to protect themselves.

Launch architecture

How divisions become deliverable programmes

The five divisions describe standing capability. The three launch programmes describe what that capability does first — and they are deliberately mutually reinforcing.

1

Reveal

Citizen-facing work surfaces the harm patterns that technical assessments alone do not see.

2

Test

Assurance work tests the infrastructure and governance controls behind those patterns.

3

Share

The coordination network turns findings into shared practice across institutions and borders.

4

Reuse

Every method becomes an open tool, so the next country does not start from zero.

Phasing

Sequenced so that each phase earns the next

Phase 1 concentrates on the two lead divisions and the evidence function — the work that produces the assurance methods, secured projects and published findings on which everything else depends. Phase 2 extends that capability outward into training and direct civil-society support.

Phase 1 · first 12 months
  • DPI Trust Lab: safeguards, identity, inclusive payments, AI governance and country trust-readiness assessments.
  • Open Digital Commons: two to four hosted open-source projects, DPG security support, SBOM and supply-chain assurance.
  • Policy and Evidence Centre: two research papers, one fellowship programme, one annual DPI trust report, university partnerships.
Phase 2 · months 12 to 24
  • Digital Trust Skills and Implementation Academy.
  • Public-Interest Cyber and Digital Safety Centre.
  • Expanded training, partner-delivered cohorts and direct support to civil society.
