Toolkit programme

Methods designed to be handed over

A toolkit is only a public good if a government team, a maintainer or a community organisation can pick it up and use it without the Foundation in the room. Each toolkit is therefore built with its own scoping guidance, evidence templates, facilitator notes and worked examples.

  • Written for the decision being made, not for the discipline it comes from.
  • Sized so that a small team can complete a first pass without external support.
  • Accompanied by training-of-trainers material where community delivery is intended.
  • Versioned publicly, with changes and rationale recorded.
  • Released under an open licence with clear attribution requirements.

Open licensing

What you may do with our work

The Foundation’s intellectual property model is built around release, not retention.

Publications, toolkits and frameworks

Released under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) unless a specific resource states otherwise. You may share and adapt the material, including commercially, provided you give appropriate credit and indicate any changes.

Software and code

Grant-funded software is released under a recognised OSI-approved open-source licence, stated in each repository. Licence choice is made per project with its maintainers and recorded in the project charter.

Data sets

Open data sets are published with a stated licence and a documented methodology so that findings can be independently replicated.

Security-sensitive detail

Where technical detail could assist adversaries or expose citizens to harm, publication follows coordinated disclosure and may be withheld, delayed or redacted. That decision is recorded and explained.

Citing our work

How to reference a Foundation resource

Every published resource carries a stable title, version and publication date. Where a resource has been superseded, the superseding version is linked from the original record.

Suggested citation format

Digital Trust Futures Foundation ({year}). {Title}, version {n}. Digital Trust Futures Foundation NPC. Available at digitaltrustfuturesfoundation.org
